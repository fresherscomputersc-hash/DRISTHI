1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT ALIGNMENT

The Odisha State Road Transport Corporation (OSRTC) serves as the primary mobility lifeline for millions across the state, operating a fleet of 1,999 buses (577 mainline express and 1,422 rural LAccMI vehicles) across 180+ corridors spanning 12 operational depots. Daily operations generate extensive telemetry, ticketing, and workshop data from Electronic Ticketing Machines (ETM), Online Passenger Reservation Systems (OPRS), Vehicle Tracking GPS systems (VTMS), and physical depot maintenance logs. However, these systems exist in disconnected operational silos.

This fragmentation leaves depot managers and state leadership with critical operational blind spots:
• Revenue Leakage: Unrecorded trips, delayed ETM syncs, conductor under-remittance, and fare code tampering erode 3% to 5% of gross fare collection.
• Fleet Imbalance: Chronic seat deficits on heavy inter-district corridors (e.g., Bhubaneswar–Sambalpur) occur simultaneously while feeder routes run at sub-40% load factors.
• Reactive Maintenance: Over 12% of transit disruptions stem from predictable component failures that exhibited identifiable pre-breakdown signatures in depot repair logs.

DRISHTI directly answers OSFIC 2026 Problem Statement #182 by synthesizing siloed data into a single, trip-level truth. It replaces passive business intelligence dashboards with four explainable analytical engines: predicting passenger demand, optimizing fleet deployment, preventing mechanical breakdowns, and plugging revenue leakage.

Crucially, Drishti adheres to an uncompromising operational design law: "Every alert shows its rows, its rule, and its rupees." Every insight is grounded in raw data rows, an explicit deterministic rule trace, and quantified financial or capacity impact.

2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & LOCAL-FIRST ZERO-NETWORK PARADIGM

Drishti is engineered as an enterprise-grade, air-gapped web console built with Next.js 15 (App Router), React Server Components, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Recharts, and SQLite via better-sqlite3 with Drizzle ORM.

Key Architectural Guarantees:
• Zero Network Dependencies: Runs entirely offline on standard government-depot workstations without cloud egress, external API keys, or SaaS dependencies.
• Server-Side Security: 100% of analytical calculations, data joins, and SQL operations execute inside React Server Components. Raw database credentials, telemetry logs, and trip records never leave the local server boundary.
• Sub-50ms Query Performance: Triangulating 135,000+ trips and 540,000+ tickets at runtime would cause intolerable multi-second delays. Drishti pre-aggregates a high-performance `trip_finance` ledger during ingestion, reducing multi-table joins to single indexed lookups that render full-page views in under 45ms.
• Pure TypeScript Analytical Core: All four analytical engines reside in pure, modular TypeScript functions (`src/core/*`) completely decoupled from React and I/O. This eliminates fragile multi-language microservice dependencies (e.g., Python FastAPI/Prophet) and guarantees deterministic, testable logic.

3. THE FOUR CORE ANALYTICAL ENGINES

ENGINE 1: REVENUE INTEGRITY & LEAKAGE CLASSIFICATION (`src/core/reconcile.ts`)
Triangulates physical cash remittances against electronic ticketing records and GPS telemetry timestamps. Discrepancies exceeding a ₹25 real-world tolerance threshold are evaluated against a deterministic decision tree and classified into five actionable typologies:
1. UNRECORDED_TRIP: GPS verifies that a vehicle traversed the route, but no ETM shift was opened or cash deposited (100% fare loss).
2. ETM_SYNC_FAILURE: Conductor deposited fare cash at the depot counter, but the machine failed GPRS sync, creating ledger discrepancies.
3. UNDER_COLLECTION: Conductor deposited less cash at the depot chest than the audited ticket total issued.
4. FARE_MISMATCH: Ticket stage distances and fare codes deviate from the gazetted tariff matrix for that route.
5. DUPLICATE_SALE: Duplicate ticket serial numbers issued across overlapping timestamps on the same terminal.
Every alert features an Evidence Drawer displaying exact GPS waypoints, ticket audit counts, conductor badge IDs, and calculated rupee loss.

ENGINE 2: SEASONAL DEMAND FORECASTING (`src/core/forecast.ts`)
Transit demand exhibits strong weekly cyclicality. Drishti implements an additive Holt-Winters Triple Exponential Smoothing model in pure TypeScript.
• Evaluates 90 days of trip-level passenger boardings to produce 7-day rolling forecasts with 80% confidence interval error bands.
• Automatically executes 14-day holdout backtesting to compute Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) per route.
• Safe Guarding ("Refusal to Guess"): Routes with fewer than 21 days of continuous data trigger a "Thin History" warning rather than presenting speculative forecasts.
• Automatically flags routes with projected seat shortages exceeding 15% of scheduled capacity.

ENGINE 3: PREDICTIVE FLEET MAINTENANCE RISK (`src/core/risk.ts`)
Transitions depot workshops from reactive repairs to predictive overhauls by synthesizing vehicle logs into a transparent, composite 0–100 Breakdown Risk Score:
  Risk Score = 0.30*(Service Overdue) + 0.25*(Breakdown Frequency) + 0.20*(Fleet Age) + 0.15*(Repeat Repairs) + 0.10*(Fault Recency)
• Service Overdue (30%): Over-mileage past mandatory 40,000 km overhaul intervals (max penalty at >=2,500 km).
• Breakdown History (25%): Highway breakdown incidence over the prior 90 days.
• Fleet Age (20%): Vehicle operating age and cumulative odometer reading (penalty for >=8 years or >=600,000 km).
• Repeat Repairs (15%): Recurrent component issues (e.g., alternator, clutch plate, brake booster) within 30 days.
• Fault Recency (10%): Workshop entries logged within the past 48 hours.
Clicking any vehicle in the fleet console displays the exact arithmetic breakdown across each factor, allowing workshop supervisors to immediately approve targeted work orders.

ENGINE 4: FLEET REBALANCER & CAPACITY ALLOCATION (`src/core/recommend.ts`)
Converts demand predictions and fleet health into tactical dispatch advisories:
• Pinpoints demand-deficit corridors where passenger queues exceed seated capacity.
• Identifies under-utilized corridors operating at <40% load factors suitable for service rationalization.
• Matches surplus vehicles from adjacent depots to deficit corridors, calculating expected net revenue gains and dead-mileage reduction.
