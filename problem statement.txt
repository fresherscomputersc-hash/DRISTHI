Problem Statements / Challenges for the osfic 2026
1.Problem Statement: The Odisha State Road Transport Corporation (OSRTC) generates massive volumes of operational, ticketing, and telematics data that remain underutilized in silos. Without intelligent synthesis, management lacks real-time visibility to predict passenger demand, optimize fleet deployment, prevent mechanical breakdowns, and plug revenue leakage, leading to operational inefficiencies. 2.Target Market / Stakeholders: The primary internal users include OSRTC’s executive leadership, operations planning units, maintenance engineering teams, and financial controllers. Secondary stakeholders consist of commuters benefiting from reliable services, state transit regulators, and technology integration partners.
